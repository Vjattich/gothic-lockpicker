[h1]Gothic Lockpicker — Solver for Gothic 1 Remake[/h1]
A free browser tool that recreates the plate-lock minigame and finds the solution for you. Works on desktop and mobile, no install needed.

[b]▶ Open the app:[/b] [url=https://vjattich.github.io/medvejatnik/]https://vjattich.github.io/medvejatnik/[/url]

[h1]Quick Start[/h1]
Almost every lock in the game is already in the built-in database — in most cases you don't need to set anything up manually:
[olist]
[*]Set the number of plates with [b]− / +[/b] to match the lock in-game (3–8).
[*]Drag 1–2 plates to match their pin positions — watch the [b]Matches[/b] counter shrink.
[*]Open the list, tap your lock — the full setup loads and solves [b]automatically[/b].
[*]Follow the solution step by step or hit [b]Play[/b].
[/olist]

[h1]Lock Database (Matches)[/h1]
The app ships with a catalogue of known locks from the game, so manual adjusting is rarely needed.
[list]
[*]As you set pins and links, the [b]Matches[/b] counter shows how many known locks fit your current setup (e.g. "31 locks").
[*]Tap the counter to open the list and pick a lock by name — it loads the complete setup (positions and plate links) and solves it instantly.
[*]Usually setting just 1–2 pins is enough to narrow the list down to your exact lock.
[/list]
[i](screenshot: open Matches panel with the list of found locks)[/i]

If your lock isn't in the database, set it up manually — see below.

[h1]Manual Setup[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Drag plates[/b] — click/touch a plate and drag horizontally to set its pin position (up to 3 holes each way).
[*][b]Amount of Blocks[/b] — − / + buttons change the plate count (3–8). Positions and links are kept when possible.
[*][b]Scale slider[/b] (desktop) / [b]pinch zoom[/b] (mobile) — resize the 3D lock view.
[*][b]Reset[/b] (⟲) — returns all plates to center and clears links and the current solution.
[/list]

[h1]Linked Plates (Groups)[/h1]
Some locks have plates that move together. To recreate that:
[list]
[*][b]Long-press a plate[/b] — it becomes the group master (highlighted).
[*][b]Press another plate once[/b] — linked, moves in the [b]same[/b] direction (green).
[*][b]Press it again[/b] — reverse-linked, moves in the [b]opposite[/b] direction.
[*][b]Press a third time[/b] — link removed.
[*][b]Press the master again[/b] — exit link mode.
[/list]
Linked groups drag together and the solver accounts for them.

[h1]Inspecting Groups[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Desktop[/b] — hover a plate to highlight its whole group.
[*][b]Mobile[/b] — use the numbered [b]1–8 row[/b]: touch or slide across the numbers to highlight each plate and its links. Grouped plates are marked green.
[/list]

[h1]Solving & Playback[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Solve Lock[/b] — runs the solver in the background and reports the move count. If a lock is too complex it times out with a message.
[*][b]Play[/b] — animates the entire solution.
[*][b]« (Restart)[/b] — jumps back to the starting position.
[*][b]Step forward / backward[/b] (⏭ / ⏮) — walk through moves one at a time; hold the button to auto-step. The active pin/hole glows so you know exactly what to move in-game.
[*][b]Solution list[/b] — every move is listed (plate + direction); click any step to jump straight to it. Use [b]Expand Full List[/b] to see all moves at once.
[*][b]Squash[/b] checkbox — merges repeated moves of the same plate into one step (e.g. "Plate 3 right ×3") for faster reading.
[/list]

[h1]Sharing[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Share button[/b] — copies a link with your exact lock setup encoded in the URL. Anyone opening it sees the same lock instantly.
[/list]

[h1]Built-in Tutorial[/h1]
Press the [b]?[/b] button (bottom of the page) for an interactive walkthrough of everything above: zooming, dragging, linking, inspecting, stepping, searching the database, autoplay, and sharing. Use the arrow buttons to move between tutorial steps.
